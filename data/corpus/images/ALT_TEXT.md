# Alt Text for Figures

Text alternatives for the corpus figures in this folder, written to the DIAGRAM Image
Description Guidelines, WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 1.1.1, and the ETH four-category
taxonomy. Decorative images carry an empty alt; complex figures open with a one-line
summary and then give the detail. Source: the expert reference descriptions in
[`../manifest.csv`](../manifest.csv).

## chem_simple_h2o_lewis.png
Lewis structure of water (H2O): a central oxygen with two lone pairs, bonded to two hydrogen atoms.

## chem_simple_methane_3d.png

## chem_complex_titration_curve.png
Weak-acid titration curve, acetic acid (CH3COOH) titrated with sodium hydroxide (NaOH). pH on the y axis (0 to 14) against volume of NaOH added on the x axis (0 to 20 ml): a buffer region near pH 4 to 5, a steep rise through the equivalence point at about 10 ml and pH 8.7, then a plateau above pH 12. Shaded bands mark the methyl-orange colour-change range (pH 3 to 4) and the phenolphthalein range (pH 8 to 10).
