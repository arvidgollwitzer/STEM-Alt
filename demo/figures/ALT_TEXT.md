# Alt Text for Figures

Text alternatives for the figures in this folder, written to the DIAGRAM Image Description
Guidelines and WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 1.1.1. **Every figure here is rendered from
synthetic placeholder data** (`held_out_report_SIMULATED.json`); the shapes illustrate the
plotting only and are not measured results. See [`../README.md`](../README.md).

## fig_convergence_curve.png
Synthetic demo, not a result. Line chart of the critic–human Krippendorff's alpha on the primary outcome (y axis) against the Direct Preference Optimization round (x axis), with a dashed reference line at the 0.70 convergence threshold.

## fig_ch_scatter.png
Synthetic demo, not a result. Scatter plot of critic score (one axis) against human score (other axis) for the held-out figures, with a diagonal line of perfect agreement.

## fig_per_dimension.png
Synthetic demo, not a result. Bar chart of critic–human Krippendorff's alpha for each rubric dimension (factual correctness, information sufficiency, domain accuracy, hallucination, conciseness) at the final round.

## fig_residual_heatmap.png
Synthetic demo, not a result. Heatmap of residual critic–human disagreement across STEM domain (rows) and ETH alt-text category (columns: simple, linked, decorative, complex); darker cells indicate larger disagreement.
