Figure 1: Overview of STEM-Alt, an adversarial generator-critic-human loop that calibrates an open-weights critic for STEM-figure alt-text. Left: a corpus of openly licensed STEM figures, stratified by four STEM domains and four alt-text categories, split into a calibration set and a held-out evaluation set. Center (inner loop): a frozen closed-API generator G (GPT-4o, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, Gemini 2.5 Pro) drafts alt-text; a trainable open-weights critic C (LLaVA-Next-7B with LoRA adapters) scores it on a five-dimension rubric (factual correctness, information sufficiency, domain accuracy, hallucination, conciseness) and returns a critique; G revises against the critique; this generate-critique-revise cycle repeats for k = 5 rounds, with a hallucination gate rejecting unsafe candidates. Outer loop: two human raters, a domain expert and a screen-reader user, give authoritative ratings that, every 30 figures, fine-tune the critic's LoRA adapters via Direct Preference Optimization. Right: the loop stops when critic-human agreement, Krippendorff's alpha, reaches the target threshold of 0.7 on the held-out split, yielding a calibrated open-weights critic.
